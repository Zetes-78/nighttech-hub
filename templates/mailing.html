Before you send an email

  • Only email people who agreed. Every person here has a recorded date and note showing how they agreed.
  • Every email needs a working unsubscribe link and your shop's real postal address (a PO box or mail drop works if you keep your home address private).
  • Honor unsubscribes quickly. Click Unsubscribe here the moment someone asks, and also in your email service.
  • Keep subject lines honest, and make it clear when an email is an offer.
